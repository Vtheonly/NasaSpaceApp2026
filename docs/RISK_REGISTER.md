# Risk Register

Review this register at each milestone. Risks below are initial categories to assess, not assertions that an incident has occurred.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation / response | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-001 | Challenge or judging criteria misunderstood | Medium | High | Verify official prompt and rubric; maintain traceability matrix | TBD | Open |
| R-002 | Scope too broad for available time | High | High | Prioritize one complete P0 workflow; defer P2 items | TBD | Open |
| R-003 | Chosen data unavailable, stale, or incomplete | Medium | High | Validate access early; record provenance and fallback | TBD | Open |
| R-004 | License or attribution requirements missed | Medium | High | Review terms before use; keep source registry | TBD | Open |
| R-005 | External service or network fails during demo | Medium | High | Test failure behavior; prepare permitted cached/sample fallback | TBD | Open |
| R-006 | Environment setup cannot be reproduced | Medium | High | Pin dependencies; test clean setup; document smoke test | TBD | Open |
| R-007 | Product claims exceed validation evidence | Medium | High | Map claims to evidence; publish limitations and metrics | TBD | Open |
| R-008 | Secrets or sensitive data committed | Low/Medium | High | Secret scanning, safe examples, least privilege, review history | TBD | Open |
| R-009 | Core workflow has untested edge cases | Medium | High | Define negative tests and acceptance criteria before completion | TBD | Open |
| R-010 | Accessibility or target-device issues discovered late | Medium | Medium | Define target environment; test keyboard, contrast, and responsiveness early | TBD | Open |
| R-011 | Required submission asset or rule missed | Medium | High | Verify official checklist and deadline; assign one owner | TBD | Open |
| R-012 | Team ownership or availability unclear | Medium | Medium | Assign one directly responsible owner per P0 issue | TBD | Open |

## Risk scoring

After team review, use a consistent likelihood and impact scale (Low/Medium/High). Add a response deadline for high-impact risks. Record evidence and close a risk only when the mitigation has been verified; otherwise mark it accepted with an explicit rationale.
